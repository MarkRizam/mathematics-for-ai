# AIR-002 experimental protocol
Educational numerical mathematics, not novel research. Before execution, fix seeds 11,22,33 and the configurations in the experiment runner. Compare hand-coded methods with analytic answers or NumPy/SciPy references. Record every configuration and seed. Evaluate relative reconstruction residuals, orthogonality, derivative absolute error, optimization objective gaps, and autodiff gradient error. Compare two runs at absolute tolerance 1e-10; exclude environment and timing. Synthetic matrices and functions only. No claims of general superiority. Finite differences should show truncation/roundoff tradeoffs; ill-conditioned solves may amplify error; optimization behavior depends on learning rate and update count. LU and QR use small dense matrices; eigen/SVD use NumPy as reference-backed demonstrations, not independent solvers. Adam/momentum use fixed iterations, not equivalent computational budgets.

Post-run verification note (separate from same-environment protocol): committed-result checks permit absolute tolerances of 1e-8 for condition 1e8 solves and 3e-9 / 3e-7 for h=1e-7 / 1e-9 finite differences, reflecting expected numerical sensitivity across environments. These bounds were selected before hosted CI; no hosted result is yet claimed.
